About this atlas

The structural access index combines three antecedent domains, insurance coverage (Census SAHIE), obstetrician-gynecologist supply (HRSA AHRF), and geographic isolation (Census geometry). The prevention gap is the cervical-screening shortfall below the Healthy People 2030 target (CDC PLACES). The priority score is an equal blend of the gap and access percentiles; double-burden counties fall in the worst quintile of both. Cervical-cancer incidence and mortality (NCI State Cancer Profiles) are shown for context and were never inputs to the index.

This atlas is a screening-and-access targeting aid, not an estimate of individual cancer risk. Many small, rural counties have suppressed cancer rates. Alaska and Hawaii are omitted.

Tolulope A. Oladeji, Digital Epidemiology Laboratory, University of Cincinnati.
Sources: Census SAHIE, HRSA AHRF, Census TIGER/Line, CDC PLACES, CDC/ATSDR SVI, NCI State Cancer Profiles.
